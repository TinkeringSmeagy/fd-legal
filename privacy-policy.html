1. Plain-English Summary

TCG Collector's Vault is built on a Zero-PII, Offline-First architecture so collectors and players can organize their trading card collections, build decks, and track trade/wish lists privately on their own devices.

  • No accounts or logins: You do not create an account, username, or password.
  • No ads or trackers: We do not include advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with data brokers, advertisers, or third parties.
  • Your data stays on your device: Your card collection, decks, storage locations, notes, and camera/gallery card scans stay on your phone or tablet.
  • You control bug reports: Diagnostic logs are stored locally with personal details scrubbed and are never sent anywhere unless you explicitly tap to export and share them.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or have access to:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or user accounts;
  • Precise or approximate GPS location, contacts, calendar data, or call logs;
  • Advertising IDs (IDFA / AAID), device fingerprints, or cross-app tracking identifiers;
  • Photos, camera frames, or scanned images from your device (all image recognition happens locally on-device);
  • Purchase histories, payment information, or financial credentials;
  • Usage telemetry, behavioral profiles, or automated crash uploads.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

When you use TCG Collector's Vault, the following information is created and stored exclusively in local storage on your device (inside the app's private SQLite database and app sandbox):

  1. Collection & Deck Data: Cards you add to your library, quantities, card condition ratings, foil/finish selections, language, storage binder/box labels, trade/wishlist flags, custom decks, and optional notes you enter.
  2. Card Catalog & Cached Images: Public trading card metadata (names, rules text, set details, legalities, Oracle rulings, and reference market prices) and card images downloaded directly to your device so the app works offline.
  3. App Preferences: Theme, display, and sync preferences (such as Wi-Fi-only catalog updates).
  4. Local Diagnostic Logs (Opt-In Sharing Only): To help troubleshoot errors, the app maintains a size-capped, rotating local error log on your device. Automated scrubbing removes file-system user paths, email patterns, IP addresses, and tokens before writing to disk. These logs never leave your device automatically. They are only transmitted if you explicitly choose "Export / Share Diagnostic Log" and manually select an app (such as email) to send them to our support team.

Because we do not maintain user servers, we cannot see, recover, or remotely access your collection or decks. If you uninstall the app or use the in-app Delete All Local Data control without exporting a backup first, your local data is permanently removed from your device.

4. Device Permissions & Why We Request Them

TCG Collector's Vault requests only the minimum device permissions required for core features:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
Camera (Optional, requested when scanning) Scan physical trading cards to match titles, set codes, and collector numbers. Processed 100% on-device using local Optical Character Recognition (Google ML Kit on Android / Apple Vision on iOS). Camera frames and images are never uploaded to our servers or third-party cloud APIs.
Photo Picker / Selected Photos (Optional, requested when choosing a photo) Select an existing photo from your device to identify a card. Uses the OS privacy-preserving Photo Picker (only the photo you select is readable by the app). Processed 100% on-device; never uploaded or retained externally.
Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Download public card catalog updates, card images, and check connectivity/Wi-Fi state. Connects only to public card data endpoints listed in Section 5 over encrypted HTTPS/TLS.

5. Third-Party Network Requests & External Links

A. Public Card Catalog & Imagery (Scryfall)

To keep card sets, rules text, legalities, and reference prices up to date for offline use, the app makes direct HTTPS requests from your device to Scryfall (api.scryfall.com and cards.scryfall.io):

  • What is sent: Standard HTTPS request metadata (your device's IP address as required by internet routing, plus a generic, non-identifying application User-Agent header such as TCGCollectorsVault/[Version]). We do not send device IDs, user IDs, your collection list, or search history to Scryfall.
  • Third-party policy: Scryfall's handling of standard web/API request logs is governed by Scryfall's own Privacy Policy (https://scryfall.com/privacy).

B. External Official Rules & Resources

Where official rules or external resources cannot be embedded directly in the app, TCG Collector's Vault provides links that open in your device's web browser (for example, official game rules hosted by Wizards of the Coast or other respective publishers). Once you leave the app, those websites are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

C. Support Correspondence

If you voluntarily email tinkering.smeagy@gmail.com or attach an exported diagnostic log to report a bug, we will use your email address and the information you choose to send solely to respond to and resolve your support request and maintain troubleshooting context, and we will delete it within 24 months of resolution, or earlier upon your request.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

  • Intended Audience: TCG Collector's Vault is currently intended for users aged 13 and older (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).
  • Zero-PII / Privacy-by-Design Safeguard: Regardless of user age, the app is intentionally engineered with Zero-PII architecture—it collects no personal information, runs no behavioral advertising or analytics SDKs, creates no user accounts, and includes no in-app user-to-user messaging or online trading.
  • COPPA Compliance (United States): Because we do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under 13 (as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506), no verifiable parental consent flow is triggered by app usage. If a parent or guardian believes a child under 13 has voluntarily emailed us personal information (such as via a support request), contact tinkering.smeagy@gmail.com and we will promptly delete that correspondence.
  • Future Audience Changes: If this policy or app distribution target is later expanded to a general audience including users under 13, the same Zero-PII technical prohibitions remain enforced by DEVELOPMENT_STANDARDS.md.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR / UK GDPR (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • Legal Basis & Processing: All collection, deck, and scan processing occurs locally on your device under your sole control. We do not process your personal data on backend servers or engage in automated decision-making or profiling.
  • Right of Access & Data Portability (Arts. 15 & 20): You can view and export your entire local collection and deck data in open JSON/CSV formats at any time using the in-app Export Data feature.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten", Art. 17): You can erase all app data instantly via Lore → Privacy, Backup & Diagnostics → Delete All Local Data or by uninstalling the app. If you sent a support email, write to tinkering.smeagy@gmail.com to request deletion of your email thread.
  • International Data Transfers: Because we do not collect or store your personal data on servers, we do not transfer your personal information across borders. (Public catalog downloads from Scryfall are initiated directly by your device.)
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to contact your local data protection supervisory authority if you have concerns about your privacy rights.

8. Your Rights Under California Law (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Categories Collected: Fantasy Designs does not collect personal information or sensitive personal information through the app's normal offline-first operation.
  • No Sale or Sharing: We do not "sell" personal information or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to Know, Delete, and Correct: You have direct local control to view, edit, export, and delete all data stored inside the app at any time. For any voluntary support emails you sent us, you may request disclosure or deletion by contacting tinkering.smeagy@gmail.com.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any privacy rights.

9. Security & Retention

  • All network connections used for public card catalog and image updates require HTTPS (TLS 1.2+); cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled at the OS configuration level.
  • Downloaded catalog updates and user backup imports undergo strict schema, file-size, and path validation before entering the local database.
  • Local data remains on your device for as long as you keep the app installed and choose not to delete it. Diagnostic logs automatically rotate and cap disk usage locally. Voluntary support emails are retained only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the ticket and deleted within 24 months of resolution, or earlier upon your request.
